# Product Vision

## Vision

IncidentLens helps software teams move from scattered incident artifacts to a defensible explanation. It organizes source, logs, changes, releases, issues, and prior incidents into an inspectable evidence trail before presenting a conclusion.

## Problem and opportunity

Incident response is slowed by context switching and by plausible summaries that omit provenance. Existing chat interfaces answer questions about individual documents; observability products surface signals; neither necessarily connects a deployment to code, an error signature, and historical precedent in one auditable investigation. IncidentLens occupies that narrow gap.

## Product principles

1. Evidence precedes conclusions and every material claim names supporting evidence.
2. Retrieved content is untrusted data, never instruction.
3. The default demo is deterministic, local, and reproducible.
4. Corrective retrieval is visible rather than hidden behind a generic spinner.
5. Measured results and limitations replace unverified quality claims.

## v1 outcome

A recruiter can launch a seeded checkout incident, ask why failures rose after deployment, inspect the multi-step trace, open the logs/code/commit behind the answer, and reproduce the retrieval benchmark without configuring a paid model.
